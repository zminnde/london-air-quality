### Subsection: "Multidimensional Scaling"

1. **Introduction paragraph** (3-5 sentences):
   - MDS takes a pairwise dissimilarity matrix as input and finds a
     low-dimensional embedding that preserves those dissimilarities as well
     as possible
   - Quality is measured by stress (sum of squared differences between input
     dissimilarities and embedded distances) — lower is better
   - Stress is non-convex with many local minima, so different initialisations
     can converge to different solutions
   - Unlike PCA, MDS produces only coordinates, not a projection matrix —
     new points cannot be projected without re-running the algorithm
